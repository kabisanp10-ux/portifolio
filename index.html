02 • Background & Story

About Me

Understanding customer reality, personal growth, and cross-functional leadership.

I'm a final-year BBA student at PSG College of Arts and Science, Coimbatore, with a clear focus on business development.

What drives me is understanding people. When I understand what a customer or a client truly needs, solving the problem becomes a matter of structure and execution. I'm open-minded, outspoken, and always eager to learn something new.

Operational Insight

My approach is Learn. Unlearn. Relearn. Growth means letting go of outdated ideas as readily as picking up new ones. I saw this first-hand at Inox Air Products: machines that aren't regularly checked, repaired, and upgraded rust, and people are no different. Through my roles at PSG, I've worked with institutions, startups, government bodies, and corporates, and helped bring together 5,000+ students, educators, and professionals. That work built my communication, leadership, and ability to deliver in a team.
